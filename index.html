Account Deletion Request

This page explains how you can request deletion of your ComoFood account and all associated data.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the ComoFood app
  2. Go to Profile or Settings
  3. Tap on Delete Account (if available)
  4. Confirm your request

If In-App Deletion Is Not Available

You can request account deletion by contacting our support team.

📧 Email: sahadadh653@gmail.com

Please include your registered phone number or user ID in your email so we can identify your account.

Data Deletion Timeline

Once your request is received, your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 7–30 days, except where retention is required by law or for security purposes.
